# The Water Cycle

Evaporation is when the sun heats water in oceans and lakes and turns it into water vapour that rises into the air.

Condensation happens when water vapour cools high in the atmosphere and forms tiny droplets, which gather into clouds.

Precipitation is water falling from clouds as rain, snow, sleet or hail. Collection is when that water gathers in rivers, lakes, oceans and underground, and the cycle starts again.
